HuddleWrist

A quarterback's play sheet on the wrist — a digital upgrade to the traditional paper wristband.

Available for Apple Watch (watchOS) · Android version coming soon

About the App

HuddleWrist is a standalone smartwatch app that displays your football playbook as clear, color-coded diagrams directly on your wrist. Designed for flag and touch football, it features 24 plays with labeled player positions, route lines, and blocking assignments — all viewable with a tap and a swipe.

  • 24 pre-loaded plays covering run, pass, option, and trick concepts
  • Color-coded routes: cyan (primary), white (secondary), red (blocking), dashed (motion)
  • Scrollable play list with Digital Crown support
  • Swipe through plays on the detail screen
  • Fully offline — no internet required
  • No account, no sign-up, no data collection

FAQ

Do I need my phone nearby?

No. HuddleWrist is a standalone watch app — it works without a phone connection.

Does the app use the internet?

No. All 24 plays are built into the app. It works completely offline with no network requests.

Can I add my own plays?

Not yet — custom play support is on the roadmap for a future update.

Which devices are supported?

Apple Watch Series 3 and later running watchOS 8.0+. An Android Wear OS version is in development.
