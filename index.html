About

Hello!

I bring ideas from concept to final product. I designed and published an Apple Watch app for football plays, helping quarterbacks replace traditional paper wristbands with digital diagrams. At Tesla, I led seating comfort designs that improved the experience for drivers and passengers on the road. Through root cause analysis and cross-functional collaboration with software and electrical teams, we made the impossible possible.

What makes me unique? To be honest, I don't need to stand out. I love being myself, and I love building and prototyping prosthetics for individuals with disabilities. My interests are biomechanical engineering and product design, and those are my strengths to help drive a team forward. A company like Apple is where my fit would be. Tesla gave me the foundation to be a good engineer.
