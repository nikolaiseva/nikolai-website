HuddleWrist Padded Bumper — a shock absorber for the Apple Watch

Quarterbacks take hits, and the watch on their wrist takes them too. I designed a padded TPU bumper that wraps the Apple Watch running HuddleWrist, absorbing impact at the edges and holding a raised lip above the screen so the glass never meets the turf first.

On the Wrist

Fit check on an Apple Watch Series 3 (38 mm) running HuddleWrist. The screen stays fully readable for the play call.

Design Specs

Fits
Apple Watch Series 3, 38 mm aluminum (case 38.60 × 33.30 × 11.40 mm)
Bumper size
38.80 × 44.10 × 12.90 mm, about 3 g
Screen stand-off
Front lip stands 1.50 mm above the cover glass and overhangs the glass edge by 1.00 mm all around
Wall
2.50 mm TPU wall with 0.25 mm fit clearance per side
Material
TPU, Shore 95A: flexible enough to soak up impact, stiff enough to hold the watch. No metal or ferromagnetic content.
Openings
Digital Crown window (11.00 mm), side button window (13.00 mm), speaker/mic slot (14.00 × 4.00 mm). Back sensor and charging interface stay uncovered.
Bands
29.00 mm end openings; installs from the back with the bands attached
Prototype print
FDM TPU 95A, 3 perimeters, 0.20 mm layers, printed back face down
