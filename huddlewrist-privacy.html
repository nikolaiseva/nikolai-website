Privacy Policy — HuddleWrist

Effective date: August 30, 2026 · Last updated: August 30, 2026

Overview

HuddleWrist is a standalone smartwatch playbook viewer developed by Nikolai A. Seva. This policy explains how HuddleWrist handles (or, more accurately, does not handle) your data.

The short version: HuddleWrist does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Period.

Data Collection

HuddleWrist does not collect any data of any kind. Specifically:

  • No personal information (name, email, location, contacts, etc.)
  • No usage data or analytics
  • No crash reports or diagnostics
  • No cookies or tracking identifiers
  • No health, fitness, or biometric data

Network Usage

HuddleWrist operates entirely offline. The app makes zero network requests — no internet connection is required or used. All play data is bundled directly within the app at build time.

Third-Party Services

HuddleWrist does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, analytics platforms, ad networks, or cloud services.

Data Storage

HuddleWrist does not write any data to your device beyond what the operating system requires to run the app. There are no user accounts, no databases, and no local storage of user-generated content.

Children's Privacy

HuddleWrist does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If HuddleWrist ever adds features that involve data collection (such as custom plays or cloud sync), this policy will be updated before those features ship. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
